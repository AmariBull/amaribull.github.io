Food & Restaurants

What I'm eating

I am a foodie at heart who loves to try any and everything. This page details some of my favs and recommendations for the fellow foodie!

Go-to spots

Places I'd send a friend without thinking twice.

Brunch ★★★★★

Toast on 14th Street

A refreshing take on brunch with great vibes and yummy tastes.

Seafood ★★★½☆

Hook and Reel

Delicious seafood for when you have that craving for the surf.

Fantasy Food ★★★☆☆

Three Broomsticks

Nothing can live up to the expectation of the way I imagined this mystical drink would taste when reading the Harry Potter series, but this one sure came close!

What I'd order

  • Kitchen sink omelette and a cinnamon roll at Sam's No. 3
  • Rare steak and brisket spicy pho at Now Pho
  • Catfish dinner and a peach cobbler at Welton Street Cafe

Want to try

Mediterranean food

I am on the hunt for the best local Mediterranean spot so I can better diversify my palate. If you have suggestions, let me know! I am looking to try out more places.

Trini food

The Trini food I had on the East Coast was heavenly, and I want Colorado to give my taste buds that feeling again. Matter of fact, if you know any Caribbean place, send it my way!
